# 💸 Minimal Budget Company Planning Document
*(For a small tech company or solo founder in India)*

---

## 1️⃣. Business Setup (Legal + Basic Identity)
Only the essentials to make your company look official and trustworthy.

| Item | Purpose | Type | Estimated Cost (₹) |
|------|----------|------|--------------------|
| **Business Name Registration** (under your own name or proprietorship) | So clients can pay you officially | One-time | 1,000 – 2,000 |
| **GST Registration (optional)** | Needed only if turnover > ₹20 lakh | One-time | 0 – 1,000 |
| **Domain Name** (e.g., yourcompany.in) | Online identity | Annual | 800 – 1,200 |
| **Business Email** (Zoho Mail Free or Gmail custom) | Professional communication | Monthly | Free – ₹150 |
| **Bank Account (Current or Savings)** | To receive payments | One-time | Free |
| **CA/Consultant (basic setup)** | Advice, ITR filing once | Annual | 2,000 – 5,000 |

**Total:** ₹5,000 – ₹8,000  

---

## 2️⃣. Core Work Setup (You + Laptop)
Use what you already have.

| Item | Purpose | Type | Estimated Cost (₹) |
|------|----------|------|--------------------|
| Laptop / PC | Work & development | One-time | Use existing |
| Internet Connection | Work online | Monthly | 700 – 1,000 |
| Power Backup (optional) | Avoid interruption | One-time | 2,000 |
| Software Tools | Development + Design | Monthly | Free – ₹1,000 (Figma free, VS Code free) |

**Total:** ₹3,000 – ₹5,000 initial setup  
*(almost free if you already have a laptop + Wi-Fi)*  

---

## 3️⃣. Branding & Presentation
Minimal but professional brand image.

| Item | Purpose | Type | Estimated Cost (₹) |
|------|----------|------|--------------------|
| Logo | Canva / Fiverr / DIY | One-time | Free – ₹1,000 |
| Website (portfolio) | Show work, get clients | One-time | ₹2,000 – ₹5,000 (can DIY on WordPress) |

## 5️⃣. Operations & Marketing
Use organic marketing until you earn steady income.

| Item | Purpose | Frequency | Cost (₹) |
|------|----------|------------|----------|
| Domain + Hosting Renewal | Keep website live | Annual | 1,000 – 2,000 |
| Digital Marketing | Start with free SEO, LinkedIn | Monthly | Free |
| Paid Ads (optional) | Promote specific offers | One-time | ₹1,000 – ₹2,000 |
| Client Communication | WhatsApp Business / Gmail | Monthly | Free |

**Total:** ₹2,000 – ₹3,000 initial

👉 You can **start professionally under ₹25,000**  
and still have everything you need:  
domain, logo, website, email, tools, and compliance.

---

## 9️⃣. After You Start Earning
Reinvest small portions wisely:
- Upgrade to **paid tools** (Figma Pro, Canva Pro, better hosting)  
- Register **Trademark / Copyright**  
- Hire **freelancers** for overflow work  
- Spend on **ads** to reach bigger clients  
- Keep **30% of income saved** for tax + emergencies
